A 100-word villain monologue for make.fun's "The Tired Dark Lord" bounty. Malachar took the crown at twenty and the ledgers at thirty; his scheme is to hand the whole damp kingdom to the hero and retire to the garden. His tiredness is never announced — it lives in the stairs he avoids, the petitions that never stop, and the roses that ask nothing of him.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the monologue (exactly 100 words, 491 characters) to `/output/entry.txt` in the grader sandbox, or to `./entry.txt` on a local run.
